🌿 Nature Discovery Hub

Uncover astonishing facts and earn points by caring for our planet

🌳
Trees & Forests

Trees Can Talk to Each Other

Through underground fungal networks called the "Wood Wide Web," trees share nutrients and chemical signals across an entire forest.

🌊
Oceans

The Ocean Produces 50% of Earth's Oxygen

Microscopic phytoplankton in the ocean generate more than half of all oxygen on Earth — more than all rainforests combined.

🐝
Wildlife

Bees Navigate by the Sun

Honeybees use the position of the sun and a built-in time sense to communicate the exact location of flowers through their "waggle dance."

⚡
Atmosphere

Lightning Fertilizes the Soil

Each lightning bolt breaks atmospheric nitrogen into compounds that fall as rain, naturally fertilizing the earth with up to 1 billion kg of nitrogen per year.

🌙
Ecosystems

The Moon Controls Migration

Many species — including sea turtles, coral, and certain birds — synchronize their breeding and migration cycles to the lunar calendar with extraordinary precision.

🌺
Plants

Flowers See in Ultraviolet

Many flowers have UV patterns invisible to humans but vivid to bees — acting as landing strips guiding pollinators directly to the nectar.

🌿 Daily Nature Tips

💧

Save Water

Turn off the tap while brushing — saves up to 8 liters per minute.

🕯️

Lights Out

Switch off lights in empty rooms. A single LED saves ~50kg CO₂ per year.

🛒

Bring a Bag

One reusable bag replaces up to 700 plastic bags over its lifetime.

🥗

Eat Less Meat

One plant-based day per week saves the equivalent of driving 1,000 km.

📦

Compost Food Scraps

Composting reduces methane from landfills and enriches your garden soil.
